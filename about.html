I’m a Software Engineer with a backend-first approach. My work spans APIs, business logic, integrations, and data flows — systems that need to stay reliable in production.

I’ve worked across authentication, caching, messaging, bookings, and pricing flows, and I like understanding the complete flow so every piece fits together as one system. I’ve built both monolithic and microservice systems using NestJS, PostgreSQL, MongoDB, Redis, Kafka, and AWS.

My approach is pragmatic: understand the problem, choose the right level of complexity, and build something maintainable. Lately I’ve been exploring practical AI-powered applications and agentic workflows, focused on real product value rather than hype.
